Education meets support

About our platform

Creating opportunities where education meets support.

PNG Student Care Hub was established to support students facing financial challenges that threaten their educational journey. The platform connects students with sponsors, employers, organizations, community leaders, churches, and businesses willing to invest in the future of Papua New Guinea. Our goal is to help ensure financial limitations do not become a barrier to academic success.

Investing in potential

Why sponsors matter

Sponsors play an important role in reducing educational inequality. Their contributions can help students continue their studies, acquire valuable skills, and prepare to contribute to the future of Papua New Guinea. Every act of support can make a lasting difference for students, families, and communities.
